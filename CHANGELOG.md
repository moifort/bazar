# Changelog

All notable changes to Bazar, most recent first.

## Unreleased

### New

- First launch asks for a first name and the name of a first house, then offers the rooms it usually has — the inventory has somewhere to go from the start.

## 1.0.0 (2026.07.25)

### New

- Photo scan of a shelf or a drawer: the AI names, categorises and counts every item it sees, and nothing is stored until the batch is confirmed.
- A four-level address for every item — place, room, zone, storage spot — down to the shelf it sits on.
- Search across the whole inventory.
- Dashboard of what is stored, what is running low and what is due.
- Low-stock threshold per item, with a notification the moment the quantity crosses it.
- Maintenance reminders on any item, one-shot or recurring: monthly, quarterly, twice a year, yearly, or a custom number of days.
- Sign in with Apple.
